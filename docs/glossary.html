Glossary & Calculations

Definitions for every metric, and the exact formula used for each chart on the dashboard.

Chart Calculations

The four charts shown on the main dashboard use the metrics below. Metrics marked RBNZ are read directly from the RBNZ Bank Financial Strength Dashboard XLSX. Metrics marked derived are computed in the browser at display time and are not stored in the canonical data files (see ADR-0001).

Cost to Income Ratio derived

Measures operational efficiency: how many cents of operating expense are incurred for each dollar of operating income. Lower is more efficient.

Cost to Income Ratio (%) = Operating Expenses ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────── × 100 Net Interest Income + Trading & Hedging Gains + Fees & Commission Income + Other Income
Numerator: RBNZ series DBB.QIE60 Denominator: QIE40 + QIE50 + QIE55 + QIE57

All components must be non-null and total income must be positive. Rounded to one decimal place.

Return on Equity RBNZ

Annualised profit after tax divided by average equity. Measures how efficiently the bank generates profit from shareholders' capital.

Return on Equity (%) = Annualised Profit After Tax ─────────────────────────── × 100 Average Equity
RBNZ series: DBB.QIE13 Units: % per annum, as reported by RBNZ

Return on Assets RBNZ

Annualised profit after tax divided by average total assets. An asset-efficiency measure less sensitive to leverage than Return on Equity.

Return on Assets (%) = Annualised Profit After Tax ─────────────────────────── × 100 Average Total Assets
RBNZ series: DBB.QIE10 Units: % per annum, as reported by RBNZ

Net Interest Margin (NIM) RBNZ

The spread between interest earned on loans and interest paid on deposits/wholesale funding, expressed as a percentage of average earning assets. Core profitability metric for banks.

NIM (%) = Interest Income − Interest Expense ─────────────────────────────────── × 100 Average Earning Assets
RBNZ series: DBB.QIE15 Units: % per annum, as reported by RBNZ

Metric Glossary

All metric names used in code, configuration, and data files must match these definitions verbatim. Mappings from source series IDs to canonical names are maintained in config/metrics.yaml.

Financial Resilience

Capital adequacy, liquidity, and asset quality.

CET1 Ratio Common Equity Tier 1 Ratio
The ratio of a bank's Common Equity Tier 1 capital to its risk-weighted assets. Measures the core capital buffer available to absorb losses.
Expressed as %. RBNZ series DBB.QIB12.
Total Capital Ratio
The ratio of total regulatory capital (Tier 1 + Tier 2) to risk-weighted assets. The broadest capital adequacy measure.
Expressed as %. RBNZ series DBB.QIB10.
Tier 1 Capital Ratio
The ratio of Tier 1 capital (CET1 + Additional Tier 1) to risk-weighted assets.
Expressed as %. RBNZ series DBB.QIB14.
LCR Liquidity Coverage Ratio
The ratio of high-quality liquid assets to net cash outflows over a 30-day stress period. Measures short-term liquidity resilience. Regulatory minimum is 100%.
Expressed as %.
Core Funding Ratio (CFR)
The ratio of stable funding sources (retail deposits, long-term wholesale) to total loans and advances. A New Zealand regulatory metric. Higher is more stable.
Expressed as %. RBNZ series DBB.QIH30.
NPL Ratio Non-Performing Loan Ratio
The ratio of non-performing loans (loans 90+ days past due or impaired) to total gross loans. Measures asset quality.
Expressed as %. RBNZ series DBB.QIC10.
Total Non-Performing Loans
The sum of impaired loans and loans 90+ days past due but not yet impaired.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIC50.
Provisioning Coverage
The ratio of loan loss provisions to non-performing loans. Measures the adequacy of reserves against expected credit losses.
Expressed as %.
Cash Position
The absolute level of cash and cash equivalents held by an institution.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIG20.

Income Statement

Profitability and earnings metrics.

NIM Net Interest Margin
The difference between interest income earned and interest paid out, expressed as a percentage of average earning assets. Core profitability metric for banks.
Expressed as % p.a. RBNZ series DBB.QIE15.
Net Interest Income
Interest income less interest expense. Core revenue line for a bank.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIE40.
Trading and Hedging Gains
Net gains and losses on trading and hedging activities.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIE50.
Fees and Commission Income
Net fees and commissions earned from banking services.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIE55.
Other Income
All other operating income not captured in interest income, trading gains, or fees.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIE57.
Operating Expenses
Total non-interest operating expenses (staff, technology, premises, etc.).
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIE60.
Profit After Tax
Net profit after all expenses and tax. Bottom-line earnings measure.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIE90.
Return on Assets
Annualised profit after tax divided by average total assets. An asset-efficiency measure less sensitive to leverage than ROE.
Expressed as % p.a. RBNZ series DBB.QIE10.
Return on Equity
Annualised profit after tax divided by average equity. Measures how efficiently the bank generates profit from shareholders' capital.
Expressed as % p.a. RBNZ series DBB.QIE13.
Cost to Income Ratio derived
Total operating expenses divided by total operating income (NII + Trading & Hedging Gains + Fees + Other Income). Lower is more efficient. Computed in the browser; not stored in canonical data files.
Expressed as %. See Chart Calculations for the full formula.
Operating Margin
Operating income as a percentage of revenue. Measures operational efficiency before interest and taxes.
Expressed as %.

Balance Sheet

Total Assets
Total balance sheet assets.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIG10.
Net Loans and Advances
Gross loans less loan loss provisions. Primary earning asset for banks.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIG30.
Deposits
Customer and institutional deposits — the primary funding source.
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIG55.
Equity
Total shareholders' equity (residual interest).
Expressed in NZDm. RBNZ series DBB.QIG90.

Growth Momentum

Revenue growth, customer expansion, and commercial velocity.

CRPO Current Remaining Performance Obligations
The portion of contracted revenue expected to be recognised within the next 12 months. Indicates near-term revenue visibility.
Contract Backlog
Total value of signed contracts not yet recognised as revenue. A forward-looking indicator of revenue momentum.
Rule of 40
The sum of revenue growth rate and EBITDA margin. A combined efficiency and growth metric for SaaS and high-growth companies. A score above 40 indicates healthy balance between growth and profitability.
High-Value Customer Growth
Year-on-year growth rate in the count or revenue contribution of customers above a defined value threshold (definition varies by source). Measures the quality of customer base expansion.

Structural Moat

Customer retention, competitive positioning, and business durability.

Renewal Rate
The percentage of expiring contracts or subscriptions that are renewed. Measures customer retention and product stickiness.
Expressed as %.
NDR Net Dollar Retention
Revenue retained from existing customers including expansions, contractions, and churn, divided by starting-period revenue. A rate above 100% indicates net expansion. Also called Net Revenue Retention (NRR).
Expressed as %.
Cohort Maturity
A qualitative or quantitative assessment of how a customer cohort's revenue contribution evolves over time. Used to assess whether early cohorts are expanding or contracting.

Strategic Evolution

Product mix, technology adoption, and competitive displacement.

AI ACV AI Annual Contract Value
The annualised contracted value of AI-specific products or features. Measures the monetisation rate of AI capabilities.
Product Mix
The distribution of revenue across product lines or categories. Tracks shifts in revenue composition over time.
Competitive Displacement
A qualitative or quantitative measure of wins against named competitors. Indicates market share momentum.
Cost of Intelligence
The total cost of AI infrastructure, model usage, and associated labour as a proportion of revenue or gross margin. Tracks unit economics of intelligence delivery.
SBC Share-Based Compensation
The expense recognised for equity instruments granted to employees. Relevant for assessing non-cash compensation costs.
Expressed in reporting currency.

Macroeconomic Context

OCR Rate Official Cash Rate
The wholesale interest rate set by the Reserve Bank of New Zealand (RBNZ) at each Monetary Policy Committee meeting. The primary monetary policy instrument, directly influencing short-term borrowing and deposit rates across the economy. Published by the RBNZ in the B2 series.
Expressed as % p.a. Source: RBNZ B2 monthly close.
