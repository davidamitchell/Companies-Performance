Methodology

How KPIs are calculated, data sources, and the RBNZ capital reform schedule.

introduction

All metrics on this dashboard are sourced from the RBNZ Bank Financial Strength Dashboard, published quarterly by the Reserve Bank of New Zealand. The RBNZ publishes an XLSX file containing per-institution time series for capital, profitability, asset quality, and liquidity metrics. This dashboard fetches that file, normalises it to a canonical schema, and presents it as interactive charts and tables.

Several derived metrics (e.g. Cost-to-Income, RORWA, Capital Headroom) are computed client-side from the raw RBNZ series. The formulas for all derived metrics are documented below.

derived metrics

The following metrics are computed client-side from the raw RBNZ series. Null inputs produce null output.

Cost-to-Income Ratio (%)
Operating Expenses ÷ (NII + Trading + Fees + Other Income) × 100
Only computed when Operating Expenses and Net Interest Income are both non-null. Trading, Fees, and Other Income default to zero when null. Total income must be > 0.
Return on Risk-Weighted Assets (RORWA, %)
Profit After Tax ÷ Total Risk-Weighted Assets × 100
Annualised equivalent when Profit After Tax is a quarterly figure.
Risk Density (%)
Total Risk-Weighted Assets ÷ Net Loans and Advances × 100
Measures average risk weight of the loan book. IRB banks show lower density than standardised approach banks.
Provisioning Coverage (%)
(Individual Provisions + Collective Provisions) ÷ Total Non-Performing Loans × 100
Above 100% means provisions exceed NPLs.
Loan-to-Deposit Ratio (%)
Net Loans and Advances ÷ Deposits × 100
Above 100% means loans exceed deposits. The bank relies partly on wholesale funding.
Pre-Provision Profit (PPP, NZDm)
NII + Trading + Fees + Other Income − Operating Expenses
Profit before credit impairment charges and tax. Measures core earnings power independent of credit cycle.
Non-Interest Income Share (%)
(Trading + Fees + Other Income) ÷ (NII + Trading + Fees + Other Income) × 100
Income diversification metric. NZ banks are predominantly NII-driven (typically 75%–85% NII).
Opex Intensity (bps)
(Operating Expenses × 4) ÷ Total Assets × 10,000
Quarterly opex annualised (×4) as a proportion of total assets. Expressed in basis points.
Capital Headroom (%)
CET1 Ratio − Applicable RBNZ CET1 Minimum
The applicable minimum is determined by bank type and year per the RBNZ 2019 capital reform phase-in schedule. D-SIBs (ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Westpac) have an additional CET1 surcharge: ANZ and Westpac +1.5%, ASB and BNZ +1.0%. See Capital Reform Schedule below.
Credit Impairment Rate (% loans)
(NPL[t] − NPL[t−1]) ÷ Net Loans[t] × 100
Quarter-over-quarter change in total non-performing loans as a percentage of net loans. Proxy for the impairment charge rate. Not a direct measure of provisioning.

RBNZ capital reform phase-in (2019–2028)

In December 2019, the RBNZ announced the most significant capital reform in NZ banking history. Higher minimum CET1 requirements phase in from July 2023 over five years. D-SIBs (ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Westpac) face an additional surcharge.

A 2.5% capital conservation buffer applies above the minimum CET1 requirement. Breaching the buffer triggers restrictions on dividend payments and AT1 distributions.

The Capital Headroom metric on the dashboard shows each bank's CET1 Ratio minus the applicable minimum (including D-SIB surcharge where applicable), giving a buffer above the regulatory floor.

data currency

The RBNZ publishes the Bank Financial Strength Dashboard quarterly, typically 4–6 weeks after the quarter-end date. For example, September quarter data (Q3) is usually published in November. This dashboard's automated fetch workflow runs on the 1st of each month to pick up new releases promptly.

The "freshness" indicator on the dashboard homepage shows the latest period available in each data source. If the indicator is more than one quarter old, the fetch workflow may need to be triggered manually.

Productivity Metrics

Per-employee and per-customer productivity metrics are computed by the compute-productivity GitHub Actions workflow from two sources:

  • RBNZ quarterly data: Profit After Tax, Operating Expenses, and income components (already in metrics.json). Quarterly values are annualised by multiplying by 4.
  • Reference data (data/reference/): Annual FTE employee counts and active retail customer estimates, manually maintained from KPMG FIPS, bank annual reports, and the NZBA Retail Banking Insights report.

Confidence tiers: Each reference data point carries a confidence flag: 🔵 Exact (published in annual report or KPMG FIPS), 🟡 Triangulated (derived from multiple sources, e.g. Kiwibank's disclosed ">1M customers"), or 🔴 Estimated (modelled from market share proxies). These badges appear in chart tooltips on the Productivity tab. Customer data for Rabobank should be interpreted as business customers, not retail.

Denominator lookup: For a quarter ending on date D, the most recent annual reference data point with a period_end on or before D is used. This avoids extrapolating beyond the last known data point.
